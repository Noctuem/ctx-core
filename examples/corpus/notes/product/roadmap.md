# Ember — near-term roadmap

Fictional roadmap snapshot, kept short and current-state on purpose (a real
corpus would replace this whole file each planning cycle rather than
appending to it — roadmaps are a "now" document, not a log).

## Now

- Weekly review: surface the one slipping habit more clearly (current
  design buries it below the streak list; see support FAQ for the
  complaint pattern this is answering).
- Offline-first sync fix: check-ins made while offline sometimes land a day
  late once connectivity returns.

## Next

- A "pause a habit" state, distinct from breaking its streak — requested
  repeatedly, deliberately deferred past the offline fix.

## Explicitly not planned

Social features and leaderboards stay out of scope — see the architecture
decisions log. This line exists so "add sharing" doesn't get silently
re-proposed every planning cycle without the prior call being visible.
